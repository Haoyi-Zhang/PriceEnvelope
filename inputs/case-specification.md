# Deterministic scientific inputs

There are no external experimental data. Inputs are rational reciprocal-affine rows and finite synthetic graphs. All realized numerical instances, or their complete graph/codeword specification, are retained in `results/exact_results.json`; controller trajectories are retained in `results/control_pipeline.json` and `results/correlated_pipeline.json`. Generator source is delivered. These are generated falsification examples, not production workloads.

The main selection uses base seed 20260911. One-stamp generic cases use that seed; transition target pairs use base+1; two-stamp cases use base+2; support-two palette inequalities use base+3; generic palette/sampling checks use base+4; certificate-oracle cases use base+5. No seed search for favorable performance was used. Parsed comparison against the retained exact JSON fails if a future runtime generates different cases.

* **Sharp one-stamp families:** supports 1 through 6 and radii 1/4, 1/2, 3/4; 18 positive-radius cases plus 6 separately recorded zero-radius cases. Generic cases: 240, dimension and row count at most 6.
* **Sharp two-stamp families:** supports 1 through 4 at the same radii; 12 extremizers and 48 generic inequalities.
* **General palette:** nine evaluations for k,r in 1,2,3; twelve supplied-distribution inequalities; six with/without-replacement comparisons for d in 3,4,5 and k in 2,3. These do not globally solve the variational program.
* **Support-two palette extrema:** r=2,3,4 in dimensions 2 through 10 and r=5 in dimensions 2 through 7; 33 exhaustive column-multiplicity extrema. Eight independent checks use the direct stamp-tuple oracle for d=2,3 and r=2,3,4,5. Seventy-two generic support-two inequalities are retained.
* **Max-Cut identity:** every simple graph on 1 through 5 vertices, including edgeless graphs; 1+2+8+64+1024=1,099 cases. Each edge becomes both oriented reciprocal rows with center 4, coefficient magnitude 1/2, and weight 30.
* **Coloring and weighted deletion:** every simple graph on 1 through 4 vertices and every stamp count 1 through the vertex count. Independently implemented color, deletion, and stamp-partition enumerations give 285 cases in each category.
* **Fixed-stamp saturation reduction:** every simple graph on 1 through 4 vertices is joined with cliques of sizes 0, 1, and 2, corresponding to fixed stamp counts 3, 4, and 5. The 225 cases check the conflict-graph realization, the radius-$1/2$ bound, and equivalence between base three-colorability and joined-graph $r$-colorability. They test the reduction implementation, not NP-completeness itself.
* **Copy-order oracle:** nine cases with dimensions and stamp counts 1 through 3 compare the direct corner-tuple oracle with the partition-first envelope.
* **Covering arrays:** explicit constructions for 2 through 16 columns. Exact minimum row counts are enumerated only for 2, 3, and 4 columns, fixing the first binary row by column complementation.
* **Five-stamp structure:** all 15 nonconstant canonical columns are classified as five singleton splits and ten two-splits. Exact incidence identities and ranks certify the `L(K_5)` eigenvalue multiplicities; the uniform and four-star witnesses both have expected pattern count 7/2. Explicit optimal count vectors are checked for every d=2 through 40.
* **Variational grids:** k=2, radius 1/2, r=1,2,3,4,5, denominators 1,2,3,4, plus r=2 with denominators 16 and 64. Every weak composition is enumerated; there are 22 grid instances, not global optimization benchmarks.
* **All-stamp support-two sandwich:** r=4 through 8 and radii 1/4, 1/2, 3/4; 15 exact checks. Each constructs the claimed q_r canonical columns, verifies every diagonal pair has two patterns and every distinct pair has all four patterns, evaluates the beta witness, checks the theorem sandwich, and confirms exact agreement with the closed forms when r=4 or 5.
* **Certificates:** the four-coordinate worked model, its accepted separator certificate, 20 concrete corruptions, and three boundary models. In addition, 72 deterministic small path-decomposition models (dimensions 1 through 6) cross-check the constructor and independent checker against a third direct full-cube enumeration: the exact upper value is accepted and a capacity lower by 1/100 is rejected. A sound loose root is accepted; a below-bound capacity is rejected.
* **Transitions:** 48 feasible old/target pairs with 1 through 4 sources and every within-phase permutation pair, totaling 7,404 application orders. Stale epochs, duplicate applications, and premature increases are rejected.
* **Generic contraction:** four prescribed read-age schedules, each 120 updates. This checks the indexing/level inequality, not the complete network pipeline.
* **Correlation-aware command pipeline:** complete support-two family with d=3, theta=1/2, three stamps, twelve unit-weight sources, and capacity 21. A direct oracle checks all 512 stamp triples and obtains M_3=21 versus R=24. Eight cyclic Gray-code epochs exhaust 65,536 reduction/increase subset states. The global-complement mixing control has load 24; rowwise attenuation is 7/8.
* **Two-link pipeline:** A=((1,0,1),(0,1,1)), w=(4,4,1), c=(5,5), price box [4/5,1]^2, optimum (9/10,9/10), step 1/8. Twelve 64-slot runs combine D=0,1,2, two starts, and fixed/refining grids. All source versions, six phase applications per target, rational errors, and rate bounds are retained: 768 targets and 4,608 intermediate commanded states.

The intake pilot predates the expanded suite. It checked 15 sharp families, 75 small graph identities, and two-source mixing/scalar-bound negative controls. Its measured record remains `pilot.json`; timing fields are not exact-reproduction targets.

The full suite is a bounded falsification tool. Successful execution is not a machine-checked proof, exhaustive branch coverage, practical network evidence, or an independent review.
